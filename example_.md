## What changed from the founder's original draft
- The "30–40% reduction" figure was not a measured/tested result — it
  was told to the founder in person by one customer. Reworded so it
  reads as a specific customer's account, not a brand-wide guarantee
- Removed emoji and exclamation marks to match premium & minimal voice
- Tightened bullet points to be shorter and more confident
- Kept every real fact: ingredients, sizes, and prices unchanged
